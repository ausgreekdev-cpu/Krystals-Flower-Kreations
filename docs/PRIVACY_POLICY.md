## 1. Who we are
Krystal’s Flower Kreations (Perth WA, ABN [XX XXX XXX XXX]) — custom paper florist + armature art workshop (Cricut + origami). App: `com.krystalsflowerkreations.shop`.

## 2. What we collect
- **You provide:** name, email, phone, shipping address, order/cart notes, workshop booking details, photos you choose to upload.
- **Auto:** device type/OS, app version, crash logs, purchase history, shipping zone.
- **Payment:** We do not store card numbers. When enabled, Stripe processes payments (cards/Afterpay/ApplePay/GooglePay) and handles PCI compliance. Manual orders (cash/bank transfer/pickup) hold no card data.
- **Camera/Photos:** Only when you grant — to scan QR workshop/custom-order tickets (`KFK-T-…`) or attach bouquet photos. Deny and QR manual entry still works.

## 3. Why we collect
Fulfil paper bloom orders, calculate GST/shipping (Perth metro/regional/national), manage custom order Kanban (Drafting → Cricut → Folding → QC → Dispatched), workshop seat + QR check-in, low-stock alerts for cardstock/wire, support and receipts (ABN invoices), and Instagram/Facebook Shop sync (if you connect).

## 4. Sharing
- Supabase (Postgres + Realtime + Storage, ap-southeast-2 Sydney), hosting (Vercel/Fly), email SMTP — as needed to run the shop.
- Meta Catalog only if you opt to sync products to Facebook/Instagram Shop.
- No selling of data. No ad SDKs.

## 5. Retention & Deletion
Orders, invoices (7 years per ATO), workshop bookings until you request deletion. Email `krystal@krystalsflowerkreations.com.au` or via app Settings → “Delete account & data” (we provide in-app deletion flow). Deletion within 30 days except legal retention.

## 6. Security & Location
Transit encrypted (TLS). Postgres in Sydney. Perth WA law (Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), APPs). If outside AU, consent to AU processing.

## 7. Children
Not directed to <16. Do not use if under 13.

## 8. Rights & Contact
