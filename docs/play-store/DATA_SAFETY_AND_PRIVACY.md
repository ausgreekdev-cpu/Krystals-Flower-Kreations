# Data Safety & Privacy — Krystal's Flower Kreations

## Data collected
- Account info: name, email, phone (optional)
- Purchase history: orders, cart, payment method (no card data stored; manual/EFTPOS/bank transfer)
- App activity: product views, workshop bookings
- Device identifiers: Expo push token for notifications

## Data not collected
- Location
- Contacts
- Photos (only if user chooses to upload/share)
